Research Objectives & Technical Scope

01

Freshness Recognition

Image-based food identification via MobileNetV2 / EfficientNet trained on Food-101 dataset with color-temperature freshness indexing.

02

Spoilage Prediction (XGBoost)

XGBoost classifier trained on IoT sensor telemetry (temp, humidity, days stored, ethylene gas) achieving calibrated F1-score: 0.89.

03

Open Food Facts Metadata

Real-time API & barcode integration for packaging recyclability, Nutri-Score, Eco-Score, and expiry heuristics.

04

Waste Analytics Dashboard

Dynamic visualization of food saved (kg), financial savings ($), and carbon footprint reductions aligned with UN SDG 12.3.
